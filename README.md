# Hospital Emergency Room (ER) Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This Power BI project focuses on analyzing Hospital Emergency Room (ER) operations, including patient flow, waiting time, patient satisfaction, admissions, referrals, and visit patterns.

The dashboard consists of two views:

- **Monthly View:** Provides an analysis of ER performance for a selected month and year.
- **Consolidated View:** Provides an overall analysis of ER performance across a selected date range.

The objective of this project is to monitor key hospital performance indicators, identify patient-flow patterns, and provide insights that can support operational decision-making.

## Dashboard Views

### 1. Monthly View

Analyzes emergency room performance for a selected month and year, including patient volume, waiting time, satisfaction, admissions, referrals, and patient demographics.

### 2. Consolidated View

Provides an overall summary of emergency room performance across the selected date range, helping identify broader operational patterns and trends.

## KPIs Tracked

- Number of Patients
- Average Wait Time
- Patient Satisfaction Score
- Number of Patients Referred
- Patient Admission Status
- Percentage of Patients Seen Within 30 Minutes

## Dashboard Analysis

- Patient admission status and percentage distribution
- Patients seen within 30 minutes versus target missed
- Patient count by age group
- Patient count by gender
- Patient count by department referral
- Patient count by patient race
- Patient visits by day of the week and time of day
- Daily trends in key performance indicators

## Key Insights

### Monthly View – August 2024

- 530 patients were recorded during the selected month.
- The average wait time was 35.1 minutes, exceeding the 30-minute target.
- 323 patients (60.94%) were seen within 30 minutes, while 207 patients (39.06%) missed the target.
- 242 patients (45.66%) were admitted, while 288 patients (54.34%) were not admitted.
- The patient satisfaction score was 5.18.
- 223 patients were referred.
- The 60–69 age group recorded the highest patient count, with 78 patients.
- The largest department-referral category was None (307), followed by General Practice (109) and Orthopedics (53).
- Saturday recorded the highest patient count among the days displayed, with 100 patients.

### Consolidated View – April 2023 to October 2024

- Approximately 9K patients were recorded across the selected period.
- The average wait time was 35.3 minutes.
- Approximately 59.32% of patients were seen within 30 minutes, while 40.68% missed the target.
- 4,612 patients (50.04%) were admitted, while 4,604 patients (49.96%) were not admitted.
- The patient satisfaction score was 4.99.
- Approximately 4K patients were referred.
- The 30–39 age group recorded the highest patient count, with 1,200 patients.
- The largest department-referral category was None (5.4K), followed by General Practice (1.8K) and Orthopedics (1.0K).
- Saturday recorded the highest daily patient count among the days displayed, with 1,377 patients.

## Business Recommendations

- **Reduce Waiting Time:** Review registration, triage, and patient assessment processes to identify bottlenecks contributing to waiting times above 30 minutes.
- **Monitor the 30-Minute Target:** Track patients seen within 30 minutes by day and time to identify periods where performance needs improvement.
- **Optimize Staffing:** Use day-and-hour patient patterns to support staffing and resource allocation during busy periods.
- **Analyze Referral Patterns:** Investigate referral distribution across departments to understand demand and improve referral processes.
- **Improve Patient Satisfaction:** Monitor satisfaction scores alongside waiting time and patient volumes to identify areas for operational improvement.
- **Support Age-Based Planning:** Use patient age-group distributions to support resource planning and service requirements.
- **Monitor Admission Trends:** Review admission and non-admission patterns to support capacity planning and operational decisions.
