# Hospital Emergency Room (ER) Analysis – Power BI Dashboard



## Project Overview



This project focuses on analyzing Hospital Emergency Room (ER) data using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into patient visits, waiting times, patient demographics, admission status, department referrals, and patient satisfaction.



The objective is to analyze ER operations and identify patterns in patient flow, waiting times, admissions, and referrals to support data-driven decision-making.



## Objectives



- Analyze total patient visits.

- Monitor average patient waiting time.

- Understand patient admission patterns.

- Analyze patient demographics, including age, gender, and race.

- Identify department referral patterns.

- Analyze patient visits by day and hour.

- Monitor patient satisfaction scores.



## Tools Used



- Microsoft Power BI

- Power Query

- DAX



## Dashboard KPIs



- **Total Patients:** Total number of patients visiting the Emergency Room.

- **Average Wait Time:** Average time patients wait before being attended to.

- **Patient Satisfaction Score:** Average patient satisfaction score.

- **Total Patients Referred:** Number of patients referred to departments.

## Key Insights



- **Patient Volume:** Analyzed patient visit patterns across different days and hours to understand ER workload.

- **Waiting Time:** Evaluated average waiting time to understand the efficiency of patient handling.

- **Patient Demographics:** Identified the distribution of ER patients across different age groups, genders, and races.

- **Admission Trends:** Analyzed patient admission status to understand admission patterns.

- **Department Referrals:** Examined referral distribution across departments to understand departmental demand.

- **Patient Satisfaction:** Evaluated patient satisfaction scores to understand patient experience.



## Business Recommendations



- **Optimize Staffing:** Use peak patient visit hours and days to allocate staff more efficiently during high-demand periods.

- **Reduce Waiting Time:** Review registration, triage, and patient handling processes to identify bottlenecks and improve patient flow.

- **Improve Patient Experience:** Monitor patient satisfaction scores and investigate areas where the patient experience can be improved.

- **Manage Department Workload:** Use referral trends to help departments plan staffing and resources based on patient demand.

- **Improve Admission Planning:** Monitor admission patterns to support bed availability planning and improve coordination between ER and inpatient departments.

- **Continuous Performance Monitoring:** Regularly monitor ER performance indicators to identify operational inefficiencies and support data-driven decisions.
